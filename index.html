The first creature

Meet SaMba

A palm-sized desk companion with a face, a name it answers to, and a memory of you. Not an app. Not a speaker. A little someone on your desk.

  • సాంబAnswers to its name. A custom wake word, trained in-house — say "hey SaMba" and it turns to you.
  • తెలుగుTelugu-native. It listens, thinks and replies in natural spoken Telugu — the language a billion voice assistants skipped.
  • గుర్తుRemembers you. Conversations carry across days. Tell it your name once.
  • సొంతంPrivate by design. The speech recognition and the language model run locally — your voice can stay in your home.

First principles

How it's made

  1. The wake-word model is trained by us, on our own pipeline.
  2. The speech stack — Telugu ears, an open-weights brain, a cloned voice — runs on hardware we own.
  3. The firmware, the face, the personality: written line by line, not assembled from a kit.

A working prototype sits on the founder's desk today and answers when called. Next: a toy-sized body of its own, and a fully self-contained mind.
