What is SaMba?

SaMba is a palm-sized companion that sits on a desk. It has a round face, a name it answers to, and a memory of the person it lives with. You say "hey SaMba" and it turns to you; you speak Telugu, and it answers back in Telugu. Warm, a bit cheeky, never robotic.

It is not an app, and not a smart speaker. It is closer to a pet with good manners.

  • It answers to its own name. The wake word is a model we trained ourselves.
  • It listens, thinks and speaks in natural Telugu (తెలుగు), a language the big assistants never learned properly.
  • It remembers conversations across days.
  • The speech recognition and the language model run on hardware in the home — your voice doesn't have to leave the house.

How it's built

The honest way: from the bottom.

The firmware is written line by line. The face and its expressions are drawn in code. The wake-word model is trained on our own pipeline. The Telugu ears, the open-weights brain and the voice all run on machines we own. Where a piece didn't exist, we built it; where an open one existed, we learned it well enough to have built it.

A working prototype sits on my desk today and answers when called. Next comes a toy-sized body of its own, and a fully self-contained mind.
